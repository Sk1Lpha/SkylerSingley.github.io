Questions and Answers

How are the products made?

Depending on the product, our products are created with embroidery and 3d printing. Our keychains are embroidered and our holiday signs are 3d printed.

Custom Orders

To request a custom order, please contact us through our contact page and provide details about the product you would like to order. We will get back to you as soon as possible and work with you to create your custom product.

Shipping

Our keychains are shipped withing 1-3 business days. In general, our 3d Printed prodcuts also ship within 1-3 business days, although some may take longer. For example, custom orders may take longer to create and ship. If you have any questions about shipping, please contact us.

Returns

If you are not satisfied with your purchase, you can return it within 30 days of receipt. Please contact us through our contact page to initiate a return.
